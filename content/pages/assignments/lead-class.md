## Assignment Overview

+ Students work in pairs
+ Students will lead class once during the semester
+ We will sign up for specific class weeks during the first or second week of class

## Assignment Details

Once during the semester you will be responsible for leading our class discussion. This means a few things:

1. You should read all the readings—including the penumbral readings—for your week carefully and well in advance.
2. You may also tweak our readings for your week by adding one additional article and possibly moving one required reading into the optional category. You should speak with me well in advance about either, as I might see something essential in a piece that is not obvious to you. _Important Note: any decision to change the reading schedule should be made at least one week prior to your assigned session, so that your colleagues have time to adjust their preparations._
3. You should come to your assigned class ready with a few questions or points you would like to discuss. You should not prepare a lecture, and I do not expect you to steer us for the entire discussion period, but certainly for the first 20-30 minutes.
